MrBenns Personal Automation

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

MrBenns Personal Automation is a private, self-hosted n8n application used to run automations with my own Google account.

It uses the Gmail API to send configured email digests and the Google Drive API to upload and manage personal automation backups. It is not offered as a public service.

Google account data is used only to perform authorised workflows and is not sold or used for advertising. See the privacy policy for details of data use, storage and sharing.

For more information, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
