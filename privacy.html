This application is a private, personal n8n automation that connects to my own Google account through the Gmail API.

Google data accessed

The application may access the Gmail permissions required to send email messages from my account. It is used only for automated email workflows that I configure myself.

How data is used

Google account data is used only to perform the requested automation, such as sending a scheduled email digest. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

Data sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except where technically necessary to operate the automation, such as communication between my self-hosted n8n instance and Google's APIs.

Data storage

The automation may store workflow configuration, execution metadata, and OAuth credentials within my self-hosted n8n installation. Email content generated by the workflow may also appear in normal Gmail sent-mail records.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for the operation and troubleshooting of the automation, subject to the retention settings of my n8n installation and Google account.

Security

The n8n instance is privately administered. OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored by n8n and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings for connected applications.
